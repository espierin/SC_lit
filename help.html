Member management

On Administration, Eric can add a member using their name and email address. New members are inactive reviewers: create a personal activation link and assign a duty period before they can review. No invitation email is sent automatically.

Deactivate keeps an account for later use. Permanently remove deletes the account and revokes sessions and activation links. Type the exact email address to confirm. The member disappears from the duty list and their assigned blocks become unassigned; Eric should reassign them. Previous review attribution remains in the history. The administrator account cannot be deleted. Adding a previously removed email address creates a fresh account; old passwords, links, sessions and assignments do not return.

Quick answers

How do I activate my account?

Open the personal link Eric sends you privately and set your own password. It can be used once and expires after seven days. Do not forward the link. Activating an account does not grant access outside its assigned review duty.

Why can I not sign in?

Your account must be active and your review duty must cover the current ISO week. Eric and Pietro have year-round access. Contact Eric if your duty assignment or activation needs changing.

When is an edition ready?

Every article must be approved or excluded, at least one must be approved, and the final text must contain fewer than 3,000 characters and DOI links only. Wait for Saved centrally. Editing an article returns it to pending.

How do I change or pause the automation?

On Administration, choose the posting day and time and save. These settings apply to unsent edition posts through Buffer. Pausing publication returns them to draft. Check the synchronization result: a failure can leave a post in the queue. Published posts and the separate announcement are unchanged.

The search pause is checked by the cloud task before research and before writing an edition. The cloud trigger still wakes at its existing time but skips that work. Search day/time fields record the desired timing; also change the actual schedule in ChatGPT Scheduled. The website cannot directly edit that trigger. The current configured trigger is Friday at 07:00 Amsterdam until changed there.

What if saving or publication fails?

Read the status message. Unsaved review text is retained in this browser. Reload the central version to resolve a concurrent edit. If Buffer needs attention, check its queue before retrying or posting manually to avoid duplicates.

Access and review

Eric assigns review duties in four-week ISO-week blocks. Use your personal activation link to choose a password of at least 12 characters. The link can be used once and expires after seven days. Keep it private. Sign in with your email and password during your assigned duty period. Eric, as administrator, and Pietro have year-round access. Contact Eric for activation or a password reset.

Check each article, edit the text where needed and mark it as approved or excluded. Changes are saved centrally. Every article must have a decision and at least one must be approved before publication. Editing an article resets its approval to pending and returns a scheduled post to draft until the review is complete again.

Publication and archive

All times use Europe/Amsterdam, including summer and winter time. Buffer schedules the approved text and banner for Monday at 08:00 on the EFI LinkedIn page. If the review is completed after that deadline, it is submitted for immediate publication. Check the publication status and Buffer queue before posting manually to avoid duplicates.

Posts contain only DOI links to the articles, never links to GitHub or the review platform. Previous editions are available in the year archive, newest first. Please keep the platform link within the Scientific Committee; article drafts and archive pages are publicly accessible.
