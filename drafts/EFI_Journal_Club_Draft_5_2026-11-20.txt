• 𝐇𝐒𝐂𝐓 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐢𝐦𝐦𝐮𝐧𝐞 𝐞𝐬𝐜𝐚𝐩𝐞
Tamaki et al. - Adverse impact of deletion of the chromosome 6 on clinical outcomes after allogeneic hematopoietic transplantation. Blood Advances, 2026.
Online: 23 Oct 2026.

Diagnostic del6p was associated with relapse after allogeneic HCT (adjusted HR 1.30). Coexisting cytogenetic abnormalities complicate attribution; reduced HLA-dependent graft-versus-leukemia remains a hypothesis.

https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2026021320

• 𝐂𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞𝐫 𝐢𝐦𝐦𝐮𝐧𝐨𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐞𝐭𝐢𝐜𝐬
Jackson et al. - Cancer-specific T cell receptor discovery using 150,000 clinical genomics samples. Science Translational Medicine, 2026;18:eaeh8192.
Online: 04 Nov 2026.

Mining approximately 150,000 tumor samples yielded public TCR candidates; 44 TCRs recognized nine peptide-HLA targets in validation assays. These selected-target results do not establish clinical efficacy or comprehensive cross-reactivity safety.

https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aeh8192

• 𝐇𝐋𝐀 𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐭𝐫𝐢𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧 𝐦𝐚𝐩𝐩𝐢𝐧𝐠
Nevarez-Mejia et al. - COMBO-RATE: an experimentally validated bioinformatic tool to identify promiscuous HLA restrictions. HLA, 2026;108:e71060.
Online: 31 Oct 2026.

COMBO-RATE inferred restrictions for 35/43 pertussis epitopes versus 24/43 with RATE; selected new restrictions were confirmed with single-HLA cell lines. Small validation samples and no default multiple-testing correction limit interpretation.

https://doi.org/10.1111/tan.71060
