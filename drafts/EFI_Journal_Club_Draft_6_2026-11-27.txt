Crivello et al. Peptide-binding motifs define the functional architecture of human leukocyte antigens. Genome Medicine, 2026;18:178.
Online: 12 Nov 2026.

Immunopeptidomics-based clustering identified 40 peptide-binding motif groups across HLA class I and II. Allotypes within groups shared more peptides; class II diversity also reflected alpha-chain pairing and DRB3/4/5. This provides a framework for interpreting HLA variation, but uneven allotype coverage and manually finalized group boundaries limit generalization. Clinical outcomes were not tested in this study.
https://doi.org/10.1186/s13073-026-01825-5
