Weekly literature highlights for the H&I community — every Tuesday.

Keeping pace with histocompatibility and immunogenetics means looking across disciplines. From transplantation and donor selection to antigen presentation, population diversity and laboratory innovation, relevant findings emerge throughout our field.

Each week, EFI’s Scientific Committee Journal Club will highlight up to three selected research articles, with an additional contribution from HLA: Immune Response Genetics when appropriate.

For each article, we will share:

• The key finding and its relevance to the H&I community.
• A concise perspective on the strength of the evidence and a material limitation.
• A direct DOI link to the original publication.

The selection draws on a rolling six-week publication window, with attention to scientific quality and a broad balance of topics over time. Each edition undergoes Scientific Committee review before posting.

We look forward to sharing research that prompts discussion, connects perspectives and informs our work.
